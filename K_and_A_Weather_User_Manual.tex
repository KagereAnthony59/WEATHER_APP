\section{Getting Started \& Main Dashboard Overview}

When you launch \textbf{K \& A Weather}, the application hydrates your cached weather snapshot in under \textbf{50ms} before seamlessly fetching live global meteorological data from Open-Meteo supercomputers in the background.

\subsection{Top Navigation Bar \& Controls}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Global Search Bar}: Type any city, municipality, campus, or airport worldwide with instant autocomplete suggestions.
  \item \textbf{GPS Locate Pin}: Queries on-device GPS hardware for pinpoint neighborhood weather telemetry.
  \item \textbf{Social Share Card}: Generates an exportable, high-definition weather graphic card complete with local background photography.
  \item \textbf{Compare Matrix}: Displays side-by-side weather comparisons across saved cities.
  \item \textbf{Settings Toggle}: Switch between Celsius ($^\circ\text{C}$) and Fahrenheit ($^\circ\text{F}$) and check update status.
\end{itemize}

\section{Intelligence, AI Narrative \& Morning Briefings}

\subsection{AI Weather Narrative Engine}
The \textbf{Weather Narrative} card summarizes the day's conditions in natural language. It calculates historical delta changes comparing today's temperature and humidity directly to yesterday.

\subsection{12-Hour AM/PM Morning Briefing Scheduler}
Users can schedule custom morning push notifications with full 12-hour AM/PM control:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Tap the \textbf{Schedule Briefing} clock button on the Weather Narrative card.
  \item Set your desired delivery time (e.g. \texttt{07:30} or \texttt{08:15}).
  \item Toggle between \textbf{AM} (morning alerts) and \textbf{PM} (evening recaps).
  \item Tap \textbf{Save Preferences}. The app configures autonomous on-device push delivery.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Lifestyle Indices \& 48-Hour Time Travel Scrubbing}

\subsection{Lifestyle Activity Suitability Matrix}
Scores six everyday activities from $0\text{--}100\%$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Running \& Jogging}: Calculated from thermal comfort, humidity, and $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ air quality.
  \item \textbf{Cycling \& Biking}: Evaluates wind speed, headwind gusts, and road wetness.
  \item \textbf{Stargazing \& Astronomy}: Evaluates night cloud coverage ($<15\%$) and moon phase.
  \item \textbf{Outdoor Dining}: Assesses patio breeze, rain risk, and temperature.
  \item \textbf{Laundry Drying}: Estimates evaporation speed based on solar radiation and wind speed.
  \item \textbf{Car Washing}: Checks 48-hour rain probability to prevent washing before a downpour.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{48-Hour Interactive Time Travel Slider}
Drag the horizontal slider forward up to 48 hours to preview upcoming weather. The entire dashboard dynamically updates temperature, weather icons, and lighting physics in real time!
